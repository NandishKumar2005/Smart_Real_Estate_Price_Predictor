Help & FAQ

Our Random Forest model is trained on thousands of real estate listings. Predictions are directionally accurate but should be treated as estimates — actual prices vary based on current market conditions and negotiations.

Currently WorthIT supports Bangalore, Mumbai, Chennai, and Kolkata with 1,400+ location data points across these cities.

The Investment Score (4 / 6 / 9) is calculated from the projected 5-year ROI, city-specific growth rate, and property age. A score of 9 = Good Investment, 6 = Moderate, 4 = Risky.
